About the shop

A neighbourhood shop, not a showroom.

Cool and Kicks is a men's clothing shop at Old Bus Stand, Rabupura, opposite Mohit Hospital. Vikash Meena runs it, and he started it so that people here can buy good-looking clothes without travelling to the big market.

You can walk in, touch the fabric, try the size and decide. If you can't come right now, send us a message and we'll share photos of what's in stock.

  • Trial room, so you buy the right size
  • Photos and prices on WhatsApp, no need to visit first
  • Everyday wear to function wear under one roof
  • Easy to find: Old Bus Stand, opposite Mohit Hospital
